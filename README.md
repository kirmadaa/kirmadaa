## 👋 About me

I'm a freelance DevOps and cloud engineer who helps startups and product teams **ship faster and sleep better**. I turn slow, manual, fragile deployments into automated, observable, cloud-native pipelines, on **AWS, Azure and Kubernetes**.

Unlike many cloud engineers, I'm certified across the **whole stack underneath**: Linux (RHCSA), networking (CCNA, Network+) and enterprise Kubernetes (OpenShift). So when something breaks at 2 a.m., I know where to look.

If your releases are stressful, your infrastructure is a mystery, or your cloud bill keeps surprising you, that's exactly what I fix.
